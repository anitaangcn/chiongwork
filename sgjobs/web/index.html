LinkedIn, JobStreet & Indeed jobs

Those sites don't allow other apps to pull their jobs. But they email you job alerts, and ChiongWork can read those alerts and put every job in them into your feed, next to MyCareersFuture.

Not using Gmail? Add alert emails as files

Drop alert emails here

.eml files, or a Google Takeout .mbox. You can also click to choose files.

  1. Outlook: open the email, click …, then Save as. Or drag it from Outlook into a folder.
  2. Gmail, one email: open the alert, click ⋮, then Download message.

What happens to these jobs

  • If the job is also on MyCareersFuture, it's merged into one row and gets the real salary and applicant count, which LinkedIn often hides.
  • ChiongWork never logs in to LinkedIn, JobStreet or Indeed. It only reads the emails they sent you.
  • Email-only jobs drop off after 30 days, since emails don't say when a job closes.
